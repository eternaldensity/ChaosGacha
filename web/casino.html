🎰 CHAOS CASINO

Broke in an endless realm of slot and gacha machines, you traded your soul for 100 coins. Casino security is coming to collect it. Pull the machines, get powerful, push deeper — and try to stay free.

How it works
  • 🎰 Slots cost coins, spit out coins + prize tickets.
  • 🎲 Gacha machines eat 1 ticket, spit out a prize (weapon, power, pet…).
  • Press E at a machine to start a pull — then walk away. Come back for the loot.
  • Every prize lands in the build bar under the HUD: weapons, powers, passives, pets.
  • Doors are tagged D3 ▲ (deadlier) / D1 ▼ (safer) — walk into gold EXIT mats.
  • Pulls are slow and loud: nearby guards come to investigate.
  • AoE and stray shots wreck machines into parts 🧩 — tinkers build rigs, turrets and barricades from them.
Controls
  • WASD / arrows — move (walk through the gold EXIT doors)
  • E — start pull at nearby machine
  • Space + direction — dodge roll (brief invulnerability)
  • J / left-click — fire slot-1 power (or swing weapon)
  • K / L — fire slots 2–3 (unlock: +1 per 5 abilities)
  • P — rotate familiars (2 active, rest stabled)
  • M — mute / unmute bleeps
  • F / right-click — fire weapon · Q — use consumable · C — cycle satchel
  • B — manage stashed powers (full slots open a draft pick)
Survival tips
  • Deeper rooms = better machines, more guards.
  • Fighting back raises Threat ★ and worse waves come.
  • Fire burns, frost slows, lightning stuns — Threat 2+ elites hit back dirty.
  • Waves shove, beams pierce, meteors land late, walls block guards — read each power's blurb.
  • Seers read doors (guards + best machine); dominate turns a guard onto its coworkers.
  • Stances reshape melee: claws feed on kills, shell thorns, wings fly. Taunt gathers, pacify keeps.
  • Rides trample and tank hits; fashion fools eyes; deep vaults hide the High Roller.
  • Ranks rise with Threat: bruisers, stalkers, hexers, medics, captains, handlers — warlords at ★5.
  • Platinum 4+ · Diamond 6+ · Legendary 8+ · Mythical 11+ · Divine 14+.
  • Death brings the Soul Slot: tier follows your depth, pulls follow your time — tickets, coins and spoils for next run.
🎲 Curse Roulette — take up to 3 curses for upfront tickets. Reach depth 3 to resolve them.
