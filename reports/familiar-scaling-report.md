## 3. Systemic pricing principles (write into Doc)

Found working — standardize these:
- (a) **Mook-vs-elite**: 100 Goons 2.5 ≈ 50 Hollows 2.5 < 60 revive-skeletons
  2.1?? — resolve the pile-up with a count×quality table; routine: mooks
  ~2–3 regardless of count, elites priced individually.
- (b) **Utility is cheap**: construction crews, transport, food, diagnostics
  live at 0.5–2.5 (Ants, Chauffeur, Chicken, Investigator-gators). Keep —
  but cap infinite economies (gold, titanite, milk-energy, souls).
- (c) **Gates discount**: needs-body (Kenjaku), needs-training (Saiyan,
  Dragonlord), nerfed-state (Lilithmon, Bucky), drawback-bound (Death
  Angels, B-1, Skullgreymon), conditional (Schrodinger-unobserved,
  Bucky-fear-gated) — quantify gate depth vs discount.
- (d) **Bundles discount**: Pillar-Men trio 5.2 < sum of singles;
  breeding pairs ≈ single +0–0.3; Cores < fixed Blades. Consistent with
  skill/trait files — document, don't "fix".
- (e) **Flexibility premium**: generalist Psyker-style > fixed,
  wish/choice > fixed (Shenron 6.9 vs ticket 3.3 needs cross-system
  calibration — Gacha-Man ticket looks underpriced next to a wish).
- (f) **Numbers premium too small for elites**: +0.5 for ×3 Fatalis,
  +0 for extra Beowolves — elite multipliers should scale harder than
  mook counts.
