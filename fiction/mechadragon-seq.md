## Abilities

### Elite

#### Blast Mark (not chosen)

Mark objects with a rune you can charge with energy to detonate at any time. Explosion power scales with energy spent; stored energy fades over time.

Source: Generic

#### Universal Pull

Lets you exert incredible attractive force to pull distant targets or bury a target under surrounding objects. Has a 5-second cooldown.

Source: Naruto

### Rare

#### Collaring

Lets you collar a defeated or submitting target, preventing them from moving to harm you and forcing them to obey your orders. Manifests as a translucent, hideable collar and chain around their neck.

Source: Generic

#### Tinker - Alchemy (II)

Allows you to build alchemy-related technology like healing, stamina, energy, disease-curing, minor permanent-buff, explosive gas, and incendiary acid potions. Stronger consumers weaken effects and vice versa, and more potent potions need better materials.

Source: Generic

#### Orchard (not chosen)

Lets you create extremely refreshing fruits and vegetables from energy, which can gain unique properties from your abilities.

Source: Generic

#### Natural Armory

Turn touched objects into stable cold weapons regardless of matter, like water scythe, stone spear, air sword, or fire whip. The world is your armory.

Source: Generic

### Uncommon

#### Burial

Instantly bury any ground-touching target underground, suffocating and crushing them. Burying a corpse prevents necromancy and revival on them.

Source: Generic

#### Invisibility

Bend light around yourself or a touched object to render it invisible.

Source: Generic

#### Lesser Curse

Place a curse by touch lowering one chosen physical attribute — Strength, Resilience, stamina, Vitality, or Agility. Potency and duration scale with your strength advantage and energy spent; only one curse per target.

Source: Generic

### Common

#### Tinder

Produce and control weak little flames, never stronger than matchsticks and candle flames.

Source: Generic

#### Genderswap

Allows you to swap your gender and the genders of those who don't or can't resist. Swapped forms usually take a shape you find attractive, partial swaps are possible, and changes can include mental as well as physical aspects.

Source: Generic

## Familiars

### Rare

#### Taurus Demons

Source: Dark Souls

### Uncommon

#### Wyvern

A large sky-dwelling fire-breathing reptile often called a lesser dragon. A wyvern can swallow a man whole and roast an entire battalion with its breath. This wyvern is female and can lay eggs to reproduce.

GLaDOS is a highly advanced AI holding immense Aperture Labs data that can interface with any system or mainframe she is installed in and build technology. She is stuck in a potato, so you must supply the technology first.
